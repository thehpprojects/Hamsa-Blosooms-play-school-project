Frequently asked questions

What age can my child start?

Our Play Group welcomes children from 18 months onward.

What documents are required?

Birth certificate, parent ID, address proof and recent photographs.

Do you offer school transport?

Yes, our GPS-enabled transport is available on selected routes.

Can we schedule a school visit?

Absolutely. Send us a message and our team will arrange a convenient time.
